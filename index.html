How this typing test works

Pick a level, choose how long you want to type, and start. The timer begins on your first keystroke, so take a second to get comfortable. Typed characters turn solid when they are right and red when they are wrong; use Backspace to fix a mistake before moving on.

  • Beginner uses short, common words in lowercase. Start here if you are still learning where the keys are.
  • Intermediate uses full sentences with capital letters and punctuation, close to everyday writing.
  • Advanced adds numbers, symbols, brackets and long words. This is the hardest prose level.
  • Code lets you type real JavaScript, Python, PHP, SQL or HTML & CSS. Indentation is filled in for you, just press Enter at the end of each line.

How WPM and accuracy are calculated

In typing tests a "word" is always five characters, including spaces, so results are comparable no matter which text you get.

  • WPM = correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes. Only characters that are correct at the end count, so mistakes you leave in lower your score.
  • Raw WPM = all typed characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes. This is your finger speed before errors are taken out.
  • Accuracy = correct keystrokes ÷ total keystrokes. A mistake still counts against accuracy after you fix it.
  • Consistency shows how steady your speed was from second to second. A higher number means a smoother rhythm.

Tips to type faster

  • Accuracy first. Aim for 95% or more before you chase speed. Fixing errors costs more time than typing slowly.
  • Use the home row. Rest your fingers on A S D F and J K L ; and return there after every word.
  • Keep your eyes on the screen. Looking down at the keyboard is the habit that holds most people back.
  • Keep a steady rhythm. Even pacing beats short bursts. Watch your consistency score.
  • Practise a little every day. Ten minutes daily works better than one long session a week.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this typing test free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up and no limit on how many tests you take.

Is anything I type sent to a server?

No. The test runs entirely in your browser. Your results and personal best are saved only on your own device.

What is the difference between WPM and raw WPM?

Raw WPM counts every character you type. WPM counts only the correct ones, so it is the number that reflects your real, usable speed.

Can I take the test on my phone?

Yes. Tap the text box to open your keyboard. Phone typing speeds are usually lower than on a physical keyboard, so compare phone results with phone results.

How do I share my result?

After each test you get a result card. Download it as an image, copy it, or share it straight to WhatsApp, X, LinkedIn, Facebook or Telegram. The link lets your friends try to beat your score.

Why is there a Code level?

Programmers type brackets, semicolons and symbols far more than regular writers do. The Code level lets you practise those keys with real snippets.
