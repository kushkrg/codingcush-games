Vyomastra

Vyomastra is a free space shooter you play in your browser. Fly up from Earth orbit, switch between five weapons, collect coins and diamonds, and beat the boss to unlock the next level. It plays on phones, tablets and computers.

Weapons and pick-ups

Destroyed enemies drop pick-ups. A weapon pick-up switches your weapon; picking up the weapon you already hold raises its power level, up to level 3. A P pick-up also raises the power level and a + pick-up gives an extra life. Getting hit costs a life and one power level.

Ships, coins and diamonds

Every enemy drops coins. Bosses drop diamonds, and clearing a level for the first time pays a diamond bonus. Spend them in the Hangar on ships and on eight paint colours. Nothing is sold for real money.

Vyomastra questions and answers

What is Vyomastra?

Vyomastra is a free vertical space shooter that runs in the browser. You fly a fighter up the screen, shoot waves of enemy ships, collect weapon pick-ups, coins and diamonds, and fight a boss at the end of the level.

Is Vyomastra free?

Yes. Vyomastra is free to play and has no purchases with real money. Coins and diamonds are earned only by playing.

How do I play Vyomastra?

On a phone, drag a finger to move and the ship fires by itself. On a computer, move the mouse and the ship follows it, and hold the left button to fire; or move with the arrow keys or W A S D, hold Space or J to fire, and press Esc or P to pause. A gamepad also works: left stick or D-pad to move and the A button to fire.

How do I save my progress in Vyomastra?

Progress saves automatically in your browser. To keep it online and use it on another device, open Account in the game menu and create a free account with an email and password.

How do I unlock new ships in Vyomastra?

Open Hangar in the game menu. Bulwark costs 1,200 coins and Wisp costs 20 diamonds. Coins drop from every enemy you destroy; diamonds come from bosses and from clearing a level for the first time.

Can I play Vyomastra on a phone?

Yes. On a phone or tablet, drag a finger anywhere on the screen to move the ship; it fires automatically while your finger is down. Tap the pause button at the top to pause.

How many levels does Vyomastra have?

Five levels are playable now: Low Orbit, with the Gatekeeper boss; Rock Belt, an asteroid field with the Crusher boss; Ice Moon, where frost shots freeze your ship, with the Frost Manta boss; Storm Giant, with wind that pushes your ship and lightning to dodge, with the Storm Eye boss; and The Hive, where you free captured pilots from webs while swarms of mites chase you, with the Hive Queen boss. Eight levels are planned, and each one unlocks when you beat the level before it.
