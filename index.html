How to get on the leaderboard

  1. Open a game and choose Account in its menu.
  2. Create a free account with an email and a password.
  3. Finish a run. Your best score for each level is posted automatically.

Questions and answers

Are the games on CodingCush Games free?

Yes. Every game on CodingCush Games is free to play in the browser. There is nothing to download or install, and an account is optional.

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can play as a guest and your progress is saved in that browser. A free account saves your progress online, so it follows you to another device, and puts your best scores on the global leaderboard.

Which devices can run the games?

The games run in a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a phone, tablet, laptop or desktop computer. You can play with touch, a keyboard or a gamepad.

What are the games made with?

The games are made with Godot 4, a free and open-source game engine, and exported to WebAssembly so they run inside the browser.
