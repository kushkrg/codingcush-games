Privacy policy

What CodingCush Games stores about you, why, and how to delete it. Last updated 16 November 2026.

CodingCush Games (games.codingcush.com) is run by CodingCush. You can play every game as a guest without giving any personal information. This policy covers this games site; the main site codingcush.com has its own policy.

If you play as a guest

Your progress, coins, settings, local scores and any profile details you add are saved only in your own browser, on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

If you create an account

An account is optional. It saves your progress online and puts your best scores on the global leaderboard. We store:

  • Your email address, to identify your account. It is never shown to other players.
  • Your password, only as a one-way hash. We cannot read it. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we never receive a password.
  • Your pilot name (up to 12 characters) and your username (a unique handle such as @sky_rider7, which you can change), which are shown on the public leaderboard next to your scores.
  • Your game progress: unlocked levels, coins, diamonds, ships, paints and best scores.
  • An optional profile photo, stored as a small picture. If you add one, it is shown next to your scores on the leaderboard inside the game. You can remove it at any time under Profile.
  • Optional city, country and age, if you choose to add them. These are shown only to you, never to other players.
  • A sign-in token, stored as a hash, so you stay signed in. It expires on its own.

Sign in with Google or Facebook

If you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Facebook", that company asks your permission and then gives us your email address and your name. We use the email to find or create your account and the name only to set your starting pilot name. We do not receive your password, friends list, photos or posts, and we never post anything on your behalf. Signing in with the same email by another method opens the same account.

Backups

We also note when an account was last used (to the hour), so inactive accounts can be told apart.

Once a day we keep a copy of the database so that accounts and progress can be restored after a fault. Copies are kept for 14 days and then deleted, so a deleted account can remain in a backup for up to 14 days.

Security records

When someone tries to sign in or create an account, we keep the IP address of that attempt for 15 minutes to block password guessing. Our hosting company, Hostinger, also keeps standard server logs.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. During a Google or Facebook sign-in, one short-lived cookie (about 10 minutes) checks that the reply really belongs to your sign-in. Games keep their save data and your sign-in in your browser's storage.

Usage counts and error reports

To see which missions are too hard and to fix bugs, the games send us two kinds of anonymous information. First, counts: that a page or a game was opened (and whether in a browser or as an installed app, and whether signed in), that Play was pressed, that the game was installed, that someone signed in (and with which method), or that a mission was started, won or lost (and roughly when), or a continue was used. These are added to a daily total and are not linked to your account, device or IP address. Second, error reports: if the game hits an error in your browser, the text of the error, the file and line it came from and the game version are sent. Neither contains your name, email or progress.

Advertising and third-party analytics

This site currently shows no ads and uses no third-party analytics or tracking scripts. If that changes, this page will be updated first.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. It is stored on our hosting account with Hostinger. The only information visible to others is your pilot name, username, profile photo (if you added one), ship and scores on the leaderboard.

How to delete your account and data

  1. Open the game and choose Account from the main menu.
  2. Sign in, then choose Delete my account and confirm.

This removes your email, password hash, online save, sign-in tokens and all your leaderboard scores from our server straight away. Progress saved in your browser stays until you clear the site data. If you cannot sign in, ask us through the contact page from the email address of the account, and we will delete it. To also remove this site from your Google or Facebook account, use the "apps with access" page in that account's settings.

Children

The games are suitable for a general audience, but accounts are not meant for children under 13. Younger players can play as guests.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: use the CodingCush contact page.
